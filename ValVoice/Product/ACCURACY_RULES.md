# ValVoice Accuracy Rules

## Core Rules
1. Never imply all Valorant agents are supported.
2. Max voice count claim is 29. Do not invent additional voices.
3. No claims about Riot approval/endorsement.
4. No claims about guaranteed ban safety.
5. No claims about cheating/hacking/modification.

## Problematic Words
Detect problematic phrases/claims involving concepts such as:
- hack
- cheat
- inject
- mod
- free forever
- lifetime free
- cloud AI
- Riot approved
- Riot endorsed
- guaranteed unbannable

*Note: Context matters. "ValVoice does not inject DLLs into Valorant" is a legitimate factual statement.*
